Summary

Wobble Tower does not collect your name, email or contacts; there is no account or sign-up and we operate no server of our own. The app does not access your device's precise (GPS) location and never asks for location permission. However, ads are served through Google AdMob, which processes your advertising identifier, device information, ad interactions, and approximate (city-level) location derived from your IP address. Where you have allowed personalized ads, this data may be used for ad personalization and measurement — which App Store rules classify as tracking. Scores and game preferences are stored only on your device.

Data controller

Developer and data controller: Zeynep Albayrak — zeyneplbyrk@gmail.com.

Data we collect

We do not ask you for information such as your name or email directly; however, the Google AdMob advertising SDK integrated into the app processes the following device and advertising data:

  • Advertising identifier (Apple IDFA / Google Advertising ID)
  • Device model, OS version, coarse language/region
  • Approximate location (via IP, city-level)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks)

This data is processed/shared by Google as a third party to deliver and measure ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising partners.

Legal basis

Personalized ads rely solely on your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); Turkish KVKK Art. 5). If you do not consent, only non-personalized ads are shown. You may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect prior processing.

International data transfer

We want to describe this exactly as it is. The data covered by this section never reaches us; it is sent directly from the advertising SDK in the app to Google's infrastructure. Google may process it in countries outside Turkey and the European Union, primarily the United States. We do not determine which country the data goes to, how long it is retained, or which Google entity processes it, and we have no technical control over that process.

The safeguards applied to the transfer are defined and implemented by Google. They include Google's published contractual commitments and transfer mechanisms, together with encryption in transit and at rest and access controls. You can review the current terms in the Google Ads Data Processing Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not present those safeguards as our own legal basis: Google, not we, is the party to those instruments. For Turkey, the cross-border transfer regime under Article 9 of Law No. 6698 changed in 2024, and we are reviewing with legal counsel how transfers of this kind — occurring through an advertising SDK — should be documented under the new regime. We will update this section once that review concludes. We prefer to state this openly rather than leave it unsaid.

In every case you can limit the transfer yourself: declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt, withdrawing your consent, or resetting your advertising identifier in device settings stops the data being shared for personalization purposes. The game continues to work in full, with non-personalized ads.

Data retention

Data on your device remains until you delete the app. Retention of the advertising data processed by AdMob is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Consent (GDPR / EU & UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, a consent screen is shown via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads load. You may accept or decline personalized ads; if you decline you will still see ads, but non-personalized.

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time without leaving the app: the 🔒 AD PREFERENCES button on the start screen and on the game-over screen reopens the same UMP consent form, where you can update your choice. This button appears whenever Google UMP indicates that privacy options are required for your region — in practice the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland. Elsewhere, you can control ad personalization by declining the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt or by turning tracking off at any time under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. After withdrawal no personalized ads are shown; the game continues to work in full.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5+ we ask permission before using your advertising identifier (IDFA) for ad personalization. You can decline; you will still see ads, without tracking/personalization.

Your rights (GDPR / KVKK / US states)

If you are in the EU or Turkey, you have the right to access your personal data, to be informed whether it is processed, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict processing, to object, and to withdraw your consent (Turkish KVKK Art. 11; GDPR Arts. 15-22 and Art. 7(3)). So that we can point you to a route that actually works, we must first be explicit about one thing:

We operate no server, no database and no user records. We hold no data that identifies you. We therefore cannot look up, produce, correct or delete a record about you in our own systems — no such record is ever created. The data processed by the advertising SDK resides directly in Google's systems, and we have no technical or contractual ability to access, correct or delete it. We state this plainly rather than promise something we cannot deliver.

For data on your device — one tap, no need to contact us:

  • Deleting the app permanently erases all game data stored on your device (scores, coins, flavors, preferences).
  • Resetting your advertising identifier (IDFA) severs the link between that identifier and any prior ad profile: Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising and Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • To turn off personalized ads, decline the ATT prompt or withdraw consent via the in-app 🔒 AD PREFERENCES button (see the "Consent" section for regional availability).

For data held by Google/AdMob, requests must be directed to Google, which is the party that holds the data and is able to act on it. Starting points: the Google Privacy Policy, Google My Ad Center, and Google's data protection request forms. If you write to us, we will share what we know (which SDK processes what data, and which channel to use) free of charge within 30 days and direct you to the correct contact — but we say up front that we have no authority to delete or extract data from Google's systems on your behalf.

If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Board) or, in the EU/UK, with the data protection authority of your country.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, sharing your advertising identifier with Google/AdMob for personalized ads may qualify as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under CCPA/CPRA. Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and other US states can opt out of this sharing by declining the ATT prompt (iOS) or the consent request, or by resetting and limiting the advertising ID in device settings — these act directly, without our involvement, and are the fastest route. For access and deletion requests, as explained above, Google is the party holding the data; if you write to us we will direct you to the relevant Google channel.
